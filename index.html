KITES stands for Knowledge Innovation and Technology EcoSystem. Our core purpose is Innovation — deep-tech led innovation, leveraging and translating research from academia. iKITES Technologies was incorporated in 2023 by Dipinder and Vishal, bringing together decades of technology leadership and entrepreneurial experience as well as shared values, with an overarching goal of maximising value addition to society. We have been focused on Artificial Intelligence, including Agentic/Generative AI and Video/Image AI (Computer Vision) — primarily building web/cloud-based software applications, and including hardware/robotics/edge-AI where required. In addition to India, where the founders and most of our team come from, USA/North America has been a region of focus for us, with significant and growing interest in Europe as well. While we began with a focus on Engineering Services and Innovation Consulting, we have actively partnered with our clients and invested in entrepreneurial/business development (IP, product-market fit, fund-raising). Healthcare, specifically Ophthalmic AI, has emerged as a major domain of interest and expertise for us — Visionary AI, our new product venture (2026), exemplifies this. Join us in our journey, and help contribute to building a better world using technology-led innovations! If we can help you in your mission, please connect with us.
