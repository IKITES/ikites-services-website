Software-Defined Vehicles on Android Automotive

The Challenge

The car is becoming a software platform, and expectations for the in-cabin experience now rival those of any modern device. Delivering a fully integrated, customisable, intelligent cockpit on automotive-grade software is a demanding systems challenge that spans hardware, OS, and user experience.

Our Approach

We worked with a pioneering EV manufacturer on Software-Defined Vehicle capabilities built on Android Automotive OS. The work reimagined the in-car experience as a connected, customisable digital cockpit — treating the vehicle as a platform on wheels rather than a fixed set of features, and engineering for the integration and reliability that automotive contexts demand.

The Impact

The collaboration moves the vehicle toward a fully integrated, intelligent digital experience that can evolve over time through software. Drivers gain a more customisable, connected cabin, and the manufacturer gains a platform that can grow well beyond the day it leaves the factory.
