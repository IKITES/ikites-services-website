Chief Product Officer (CPO) Program

The Challenge

Senior product leaders are expected to operate across strategy, leadership, and execution at once — yet few programs prepare them for the breadth of that role. Generic management training rarely reflects the specific, fast-moving challenges of leading product at the highest level.

Our Approach

We helped deliver an advanced program designed to build the strategic, leadership, and operational skills product leaders actually need. Delivered across multiple cohorts, it leans on interactive, hands-on work with real-world scenarios tailored to today's product challenges, so learning maps directly onto the decisions participants make in their roles.

The Impact

Across its cohorts, the program sharpens the judgement and capabilities of senior product leaders. Participants develop a more complete command of the CPO remit — from long-term strategy to day-to-day operational leadership — through practice rooted in realistic situations.
