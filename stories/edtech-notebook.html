Real-Time Notebook Capture for EdTech

The Challenge

Much of how students actually work still happens on paper. Bringing handwritten notebooks into a digital learning experience usually means clunky uploads or heavy server-side processing that struggles in the low-bandwidth conditions many learners face.

Our Approach

We built custom AI models that let students display their physical notebooks through a standard computer camera, with processing running on the edge — directly in the browser. Keeping inference on the device avoids round-trips to a server, so the experience stays responsive even when connectivity is limited.

The Impact

The result bridges traditional and digital learning without forcing a trade-off. Students can bring paper work into the digital classroom in real time, reliably, regardless of bandwidth — making the tool genuinely usable in the settings where it matters most.
