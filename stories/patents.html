Strategic Patent Portfolio for Light-Therapy Innovation

The Challenge

Breakthroughs in light therapy and wellness technology are only as valuable as the protection around them. Without a deliberate IP strategy, core innovations are exposed, market position is fragile, and long-term value is harder to defend and to demonstrate.

Our Approach

We developed a robust patent portfolio securing the core innovations behind a light-therapy and wellness platform. Rather than treating patents as a checkbox, we aligned IP strategy directly with business goals — identifying what was genuinely defensible and worth protecting, and structuring the portfolio to support the company's commercial direction.

The Impact

The portfolio protects key breakthroughs, strengthens market position, and underpins long-term valuation and growth. With IP and business strategy moving together, the innovations are positioned to hold their value as the company scales.
