TETR Training Program for Global Entrepreneurs

The Challenge

Aspiring entrepreneurs around the world have ambition but often lack practical, hands-on guidance on how to actually build and launch a venture. Traditional, lecture-heavy formats rarely close the gap between business theory and the realities of getting a product into the market.

Our Approach

In collaboration with TETR College of Business and FITT, IIT Delhi, we helped shape a training program built around experiential learning. Bootcamps led by experienced founders give participants hands-on practice in business thinking and product development, grounded in the real decisions and trade-offs entrepreneurs face rather than abstract case studies.

The Impact

Participants leave with practical tools and a founder's mindset for launching ventures. By pairing experienced practitioners with experiential learning, the program equips entrepreneurs worldwide to turn ideas into businesses with greater confidence.
